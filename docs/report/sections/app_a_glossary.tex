\thd{Term} & \thd{Meaning in this report}\\
\endfirsthead
\thd{Term} & \thd{Meaning in this report (continued)}\\
\endhead
Access token & The JWT returned at login and renewal; sent as a Bearer token on every REST request and on the live feed's negotiation; valid for 900\,s\\\rowsep
\code{Accepted}, \code{Working}, \code{Done} & Order states in the feed's \code{ords}. \code{Accepted}: right after placement (seen once, order 1650719). \code{Working}: in the market, possibly partly filled. \code{Done}: finished, filled or stopped. Observed sequences: \code{Accepted} to \code{Done} and \code{Working} to \code{Done}; \code{Generated} and \code{Deleted} were not observed\\\rowsep
ALPN & TLS extension with which client and server agree on the HTTP version during the handshake\\\rowsep
Bearer token & An access token sent as \code{Authorization: Bearer <token>}; whoever holds it is authenticated\\\rowsep
\code{bs} & Feed record with the portfolio's totals: net assets (\code{na}), profit (\code{prft}), return (\code{rt}), \code{nav} and stock amount (\code{stkAmt})\\\rowsep
\code{bs_updated} & Field carried by every delta (20 of 20 at 10:33) with the changed values of \code{bs}: \code{prft}, \code{na}, \code{rt}, \code{nav}, \code{stkAmt}, sometimes \code{pfStd} and \code{rank}; the wrapper merges it into \code{bs}\\\rowsep
Clock skew & The tolerance a server allows when comparing a token's times with its own clock; 300\,s here\\\rowsep
\code{cmpl} & Feed record with this week's turnover (\code{currWto}) and the count of weekly turnover violations (\code{wtoViols})\\\rowsep
\code{cnclT} & Time an order was stopped; null otherwise. A stopped order still ends in \code{Done}, so \code{cnclT} tells a stopped order from a filled one\\\rowsep
Cookie jar & All cookies the server sets at login, \code{refreshToken} among them. They expire 7 days after login; renewal does not extend them and \code{Logout} does not revoke them. The wrapper stores them in \code{~/.config/pytimefolio/session.json}, mode 0600\\\rowsep
\code{cumQty} & Shares of an order filled so far\\\rowsep
\code{currBizDay} & The server's current business day, returned at login and renewal; it rolls over between 06:02 and 07:07\\\rowsep
Delta & A live-feed reply holding only what changed since the reply whose \code{t} the client echoed (\code{*_diff} fields and \code{bs_updated}); a delta may be empty\\\rowsep
GICS sector & Sector code of the Global Industry Classification Standard (e.g.\ 45 Information Technology), carried as \code{sec} in \code{Universe}, \code{pos} and \code{msecs}\\\rowsep
JWT & JSON Web Token: a signed token whose claims (\code{exp}, \code{iat}, \ldots) can be read without the key\\\rowsep
Keep-alive & Keeping a TCP connection open between requests so later requests skip set-up\\\rowsep
\code{lastT}, \code{t} & \code{t} is the server's stamp on a reply; \code{lastT} is the stamp the client echoes to ask for a delta\\\rowsep
\code{limitIdx} & Relative price of an order in ticks: $n$ on the opposite side, $-n$ on the order's own side ($n$ from 1 to 10)\\
\rowsep
Login session & The server-side session one login creates, held by its cookie jar; it lasts 7 days, and each login adds one\\\rowsep
\code{msecs} & Market weight of each GICS sector in the feed, the base of the sector limit\\\rowsep
\code{na} & Net assets of the portfolio in KRW, in the feed's \code{bs}; the denominator of one share's weight\\\rowsep
Negotiate & SignalR's first HTTP request, which returns a connection token and the transports on offer\\\rowsep
\code{pfId} & Portfolio ID; one account has one per contest entry and reset\\\rowsep
Position record & One entry of \code{pos} in a snapshot, with 28 keys: \code{Id}, \code{avgPrc}, \code{bPrc}, \code{bQty}, \code{chPct}, \code{close}, \code{ctstNm}, \code{d}, \code{div}, \code{fee}, \code{pfId}, \code{pos}, \code{posYd}, \code{prcYd}, \code{prft}, \code{prftKrw}, \code{prodId}, \code{prodNm}, \code{rstr}, \code{sPrc}, \code{sQty}, \code{sec}, \code{tax}, \code{userNick}, \code{userNm}, \code{wei}, \code{weiLimit}, \code{yWei}\\\rowsep
pp & Percentage point: the difference between two percentages (12\% to 15\% is 3 pp)\\\rowsep
\code{qty2ord} & Number of shares an order asks for, computed by the server from its \code{wei} and rounded down to whole shares\\\rowsep
Renewal & Getting a new access token from \code{POST /api/Auth/SilentRefresh} with the cookie jar; renew is the verb\\\rowsep
RTT & Round-trip time: one message to the server and back\\\rowsep
SignalR & Microsoft's library for live messaging between ASP.NET servers and clients, here over WebSocket\\\rowsep
Small cap & A stock with a market capitalisation under KRW 1 trillion, labelled \code{SmallCap} in the feed's \code{tags}\\\rowsep
Snapshot & A live-feed reply holding the full state (\code{bs}, \code{ords}, \code{pos}, \code{msecs} and 5 smaller top-level fields); sent when the client's echo of \code{t} is missing or does not match\\\rowsep
Stop & Cancelling a working order through \code{CancelOrderBatch}; a stopped order ends in \code{Done} with \code{cnclT} set, and each stopped order ID counts towards the daily limit\\\rowsep
Trading session & 09:00 to 15:20 KST, the hours in which orders fill\\\rowsep
Turnover & Weekly trading as a percentage of the portfolio; the contest requires at least 5\% every week\\\rowsep
\code{wei} & Weight in percent (9 means 9\%); on a position the current weight, on an order the size of the order, which the wrapper quantises to 0.01\%\\\rowsep
\code{weiLimit} & Upper limit of a stock's weight in percent: 40 for Samsung Electronics, 15 for the others\\
\end{longtable}}
