\claim{Appendix \secnum{B}{app:decisions}\quad Decision log}
\lead{Thirty-six decisions determine the wrapper's behaviour, and 19 of them would cost an order, the account or a credential if they were wrong.} Each row gives the decision, the section that holds its evidence with one number from it, and the source of that evidence: Measured (our own requests on 24 Nov 2026), Organiser (the e-mail reply [5]), Competition (the guideline page of the competition site) or Framework docs (the ASP.NET Core documentation [9]). The column \emph{Cost if wrong} is High when a wrong decision would place, lose or duplicate an order, break a guideline, get the account blocked or expose a credential, and Low when it would cost extra requests, latency or a visible error. The column \emph{Revisit if} names the observation that would reopen the decision. The tables group the decisions by layer; D-numbers stay as cited in the sections, so they are not in order within a table.
