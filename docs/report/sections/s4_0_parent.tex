\claim{\secnum{4}{sec:live}\quad Once the 2 defects they exposed were fixed, the tests showed 2 of the 5 goals in full; G1 and G3 are partly shown and G4 is open}

\lead{The live tests on 24 Nov 2026 exposed 2 defects, and a code review between the first live run and these tests had fixed 7 others.} The tests ran against one live portfolio from 09:27 to 09:55 KST, with a rounding test at 10:26 and 10:33; test orders carried limits 20\% from the market so that they could not fill, and the day ended at 9 new orders and 7 stops, 16 of the wrapper's daily cap of 80. Failures the live server cannot be made to produce on demand, such as a lost order reply or a 429, are covered by 22 of the 31 offline tests. Section~\ref{sec:wrappertests} tests start-up, the feed, a buy and a sell, and finds that \code{plan()} accepted targets summing to 139\%; \ref{sec:oneshare} shows that the server rounds an order's share count down; \ref{sec:guidelines} finds that \code{guidelines()} keyed sector limits by the wrong field; \ref{sec:validity} sets out what these tests cannot show and the questions still open.
