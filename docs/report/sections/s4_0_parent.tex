\claim{\secnum{4}{sec:live}\quad Live tests passed once the defects they exposed were fixed}

\lead{The wrapper passed its live tests on 24 Nov 2026 after the 2 defects those tests exposed were fixed.} The tests ran against one live portfolio from 09:27 to 09:55 KST, with a rounding test at 10:26 and 10:33; test orders carried limits 20\% from the market so that they could not fill, and the day ended at 9 new orders and 7 stops, 16 of the wrapper's daily cap of 80. Failures the live server cannot be made to produce on demand, such as a lost order reply or a 429, are covered by 15 of the 24 offline tests. Section~\ref{sec:wrappertests} tests start-up, the feed, a buy and a sell, and found that \code{plan()} accepted targets summing to 139\%; \ref{sec:oneshare} proves that the server rounds an order's share count down; \ref{sec:guidelines} found that \code{guidelines()} keyed sector limits by the wrong field; \ref{sec:validity} sets out what these tests cannot show and the questions still open.
