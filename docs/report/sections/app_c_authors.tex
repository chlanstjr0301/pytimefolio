\lead{Author statement.} The measurements, code and conclusions in this report are the authors' own. No other participant's account data was requested.

\lead{Nature of the report.} DART is the Financial Data Science Club at Kyungpook National University. This report is an academic study of a web service's undocumented behaviour as observed on 24 Nov 2026; it is not investment advice and recommends no security. The authors are not affiliated with Timefolio Asset Management, which publishes no API and has not reviewed or endorsed this report or the package. The organiser stated by e-mail on 24 Nov 2026 that automated orders are not prohibited and that an IP address placing 100 or more orders a day, or sending bursts of traffic, may be blocked [5].
